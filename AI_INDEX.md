[assurance statement](ASSURANCE.md). The principal theorem is the bound
108 ≤ alpha(C7 strong power 4) ≤ 112, not an exact independence number.

The [main certificate](evidence/c7-fourth-power-upper112.zip) contains two
complete case components, the lower-dimensional prerequisites, exact proof
checkers and the 108 witness. The [guide](guides/c7-fourth-power-upper112-README-v2.md)
explains normalization and resource requirements. [verify_bundle.py](verify_bundle.py)
checks outer integrity and elementary controls; its --full option launches
the long mathematical replay.

- [Square gadgets](evidence/c7-gao-square-product-maximum106.zip): maximum 106 within the specified construction, not a graph upper bound.
- [Invariant moments](evidence/c7-invariant-relaxation-barrier.zip): exact feasible pseudo-moments at 112 and, by thinning, 109–111; not a physical code.
